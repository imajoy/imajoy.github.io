Basic and Premium access

Basic

Requires internet access when Maya starts to check the subscription. Updates are installed automatically.

Premium

Requires internet to install, then works without a continuing connection. You choose when to check for updates.

Basic includes the 10 Wrapline Pro tools. Premium includes all 14 tools: the 10 Wrapline Pro tools plus the 4 Wrapline tools, installed together for offline use. Both plans run on Autodesk Maya 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 on Windows. Premium access is still a subscription: working without internet does not extend the paid license period.
