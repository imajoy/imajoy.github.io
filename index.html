Export

Get shots, assets and previews out of Maya cleanly.

  • FBX ExporterExport rig assets, deformation joints and cameras by shot.
  • Alembic ExporterExport geometry and cameras with UVs, visibility and face sets.
  • PlayblastCapture camera previews with resolution presets and audio.
  • Audio ExporterExtract WAV audio from video shots and optionally import it into Maya.

  • Multi SwapperMoves animation onto locators and back, keying only the frames you already keyed.

Which versions of Maya does it support?

Autodesk Maya 2023 and newer, on Windows.

What is the difference between Online and Offline?

Online checks your subscription each time Maya starts and always runs the newest version. Offline works without an internet connection once installed, and you choose when to update from the Ajoy Tools menu.

What happens if I stop paying?

Access is tied to an active subscription. When it ends, your code is turned off and the tools stop working. See the license for the full terms.

Can I assign hotkeys to the tools?

Yes. Right-click any tool in the Ajoy Tools menu or on the shelf and choose Assign Hotkey. Maya tells you if the combination is already in use.

What else do I need installed?

The fbxmaya plug-in for FBX export and AbcExport for Alembic export. The Audio Exporter also needs FFmpeg, which is not bundled.

How do I remove it?

Choose Ajoy Tools, then Uninstall, and confirm. Your settings, presets and exported files are kept.

Is the setup file safe to share?

Yes, it contains no tools and no secrets. It cannot install anything without a valid code.
