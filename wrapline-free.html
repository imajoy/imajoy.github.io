    Questions artists ask.

    Need something else? Send us a message.

    Is Wrapline really free?

    Yes. Every Wrapline tool is free for everyone. There is no trial, no subscription and no payment, and it can’t be bought.

    Why do I need to sign in to download?

    Signing in with your email lets us send you updates and important fixes for the tools. It’s free, takes a few seconds, and we never share your email.

    Which versions of Maya does it support?

    Autodesk Maya 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, on Windows.

    Can I use it for commercial work?

    Yes, you can use the tools in your own production work. You may not copy, resell, redistribute or repackage the tools, their interface or their code. All rights are reserved by AJOY. See the license for details.

    How is Wrapline different from Wrapline Pro?

    Wrapline is the free toolkit of small, handy tools. Wrapline Pro is the paid toolkit with the larger production tools, sold as a Basic or Premium subscription.

    I have Wrapline Pro. Do I also need this?

    On Wrapline Pro Basic, yes: Basic includes the 10 Pro tools only, so download free Wrapline here to use these 4 tools as well. Both work side by side in Maya. Wrapline Pro Premium already includes all 14 tools, so you don’t need it.

    Can I review the free tools?

    Yes, please do. Open a tool’s page, scroll to Reviews and click Write a review. One review per account per tool, and you can edit it any time.

    Can I assign hotkeys to the tools?

    Yes. Right-click any tool in the Wrapline menu or on the shelf and choose Assign Hotkey. Maya tells you if the combination is already in use.
