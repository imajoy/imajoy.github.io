01 / PRIVACY

Who this notice covers

This notice explains how AJOY handles information connected with the Ajoy Tools website, installer, licensing and support. AJOY is responsible for the uses described here. For questions or privacy requests, contact contactmy3dart@gmail.com.

The public website, preview checkout and Maya activation service are different parts of the product. A feature described as available only when enabled does not mean it is already running.

02 / PRIVACY

Information we use

Website visits
GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting providers can process your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure pages.
License activation
The installer sends your access code, a stable device identifier, computer name, Windows user name, local network address, Maya version and Python build identifier to the licensing service. The device identifier can be linked to your license; it is not treated as anonymous.
Plan and country choices
The browser can suggest a country using its time zone or language. When server pricing is connected, your chosen country, license type, billing period and quantity are used to calculate a quote. A coupon code is sent for validation when that feature is connected.
Billing and support
Billing fields can include your name, email, company, address, country and tax ID. Support emails contain the information and attachments you choose to send.

The installer’s activation request does not upload Maya scenes, textures or project files. Avoid sending private project files or access codes by email unless they are needed to resolve your support request.

03 / PRIVACY

Why we use it

We use license and device information to activate the correct package, enforce the number of licensed computers, deliver updates, diagnose installation problems and detect code sharing or misuse. Plan and country choices support pricing and coupon checks. Billing information, when checkout is enabled, supports orders, license delivery, receipts, tax and accounting. Support messages are used to answer your request.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, these uses may rely on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing it, or legal obligations such as accounting. Consent is requested where applicable law requires it. We do not sell your billing or activation data for advertising.

04 / PRIVACY

Billing and regional pricing

Payments are not open yet.

Billing details are not submitted for payment while checkout is unavailable. If you choose returning subscriber verification when it is enabled, your email is sent to Supabase Auth to deliver and verify a one-time code; this does not create a payment. It does not write those fields to this site’s local storage or page URL. Your browser may separately offer its own autofill or form-saving features.

Returning subscriber verification. Verifying your email may create a passwordless customer account. We use its stable account ID and a hash of your verified email to link previously confirmed payments, count eligible paid billing periods and apply one-time loyalty rewards. Invoice references, provider customer IDs, payment status, period dates and reward usage are kept privately for this purpose. A hashed email remains personal information, not anonymous data. Authentication tokens stay in browser memory for this visit; we do not save them to this site’s local storage. Billing names, addresses, card numbers and IP addresses are not used as proof of returning-subscriber eligibility.

Prices can vary by billing country. When server pricing is enabled, an approximate country lookup may send your IP address to country.is. Billing details confirm the applicable price; any tax and final payment total must be shown before you pay.

This website has no card-number fields. Before online payments become available, the selected payment provider and its applicable terms will be identified. Payment details will be handled through the provider’s checkout.

05 / PRIVACY

Downloads and your email

The setup file is free. Before it downloads, the download page asks for your email address (a .com or .in address, such as Gmail, Yahoo or Hotmail). It is saved, with the date, in a private Google Sheet that only AJOY can open, so AJOY can follow up about Ajoy Tools. If you enter the same address again, its date is updated and no copy is added. Email AJOY at the contact address below to have yours removed.

The sheet is stored with Google. There is no human check on the download page. The installer cannot install any tools without a valid access code.

06 / PRIVACY

Storage, providers and security

The site remembers your chosen light or dark theme in your browser. It includes no advertising pixels or analytics scripts. Service providers can still process the technical information needed to deliver their services.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Supabase provides the activation infrastructure and is the intended host for pricing and verified-download services. Service logs and license records may be processed there. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and country.is process verification or location requests when those features are enabled.
  • Email providers process messages you send to support.

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Applicable protections and provider terms govern those transfers. We use access controls and secure connections for service requests; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

07 / PRIVACY

How long information stays

We keep information only for the period needed for its purpose, including active license administration, support, security, disputes and legal obligations. Accounting records may need to be kept after a subscription ends. Provider logs follow the relevant service’s retention settings. We do not promise one fixed deletion period for every type of record.

Your theme preference stays in your browser until you change it or clear the site’s stored data. Contact us to ask about records linked to your license or a specific request.

08 / PRIVACY

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. If a use relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Email contactmy3dart@gmail.com with the request and enough detail to identify the relevant account or license. We may ask for proportionate verification before sharing or changing records. Legal duties, security needs or others’ rights may limit a request; we will explain where that applies.

You can decline to enter billing details or contact support. Activation requires the license and device information needed to validate access. Ajoy Tools is intended for professional users; please contact us if a child has provided personal information.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice when our handling changes and show the revision date at the top. Material changes will be brought to your attention where required by law. Before activating a new payment or data-processing feature, the relevant notice will be updated.

For privacy, billing-data or licensing questions: contactmy3dart@gmail.com.
