01 / PRIVACY

Who this notice covers

This notice explains how AJOY handles information for the Wrapline and Wrapline Pro website (by Ajoy Tools), installer, licensing and support. AJOY is responsible for the uses described here. For privacy questions or requests, use our contact form.

02 / PRIVACY

Wrapline (free) and coffee support

Wrapline, the free toolkit, needs no subscription. Its download needs a free account, verified by email. The rest of this notice applies to Wrapline too. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

  • Buy me a coffee. If you choose to give a coffee, the support page sends the amount, your optional name and your optional note to our payment service. PayPal or Razorpay handle your payment details; they never reach this website. We keep a record of the gift and who it came from so we can say thank you.
  • Reviews. If you post a review on a tool page, your chosen name, stars and review text are public. Your email stays private.

03 / PRIVACY

Information we use

Website visits
IP address, browser information and request logs help deliver and secure the website.
License activation
Your access code, device identifier, computer name, Windows user name, local network address, public IP address, Maya version and Python build identifier are used to validate access and enforce computer limits. Device details are linked to your license. Failed activation attempts are logged with your IP address for about a day to slow down code guessing.
Regional pricing
Your approximate country, estimated from your connection, and your detected billing country help show the relevant price. Your plan, billing period and license quantity are used to prepare checkout.
Account and orders
Your verified email, selected plan, license quantity, invoices and payment records support registration, downloads, subscriptions and returning subscriber rewards.
Trial eligibility
Your verified email (ignoring “+” tags and Gmail dots), a random browser identifier, a one-way hash of your IP address and the PayPal payer identifier support checks that limit trials and coupons to one per customer. Shared networks may affect eligibility; contact support if you believe a check is incorrect.
Billing and support
Billing details can include your name, email, optional company, country and address (city, region and postal code). The contact form collects your name, reply email, topic, message, privacy consent and any image, GIF or video you choose to attach. You can contact us without an account. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or files you do not have permission to share.

04 / PRIVACY

Why we use it

We use license and device information to validate active access, enforce licensed computer counts, deliver the correct package and updates, and resolve installation problems. Account and billing information supports subscriptions, license delivery, receipts, applicable accounting duties and verified offers. Technical checks help secure the service and prevent trial abuse. Support messages are used to answer your request.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, or legal obligations. We request consent where required. We do not sell your personal information for advertising.

05 / PRIVACY

Billing and regional pricing

PayPal and, when available for your country, Razorpay handle payment approval and recurring billing. Ajoy receives payment-provider identifiers and subscription status, not full card numbers or card security codes. PayPal processes payment details under its privacy statement.

Private account and order records include your verified email, name, optional company, billing address (street, city, region, postal code), country, plan, quantity, an Order ID and subscription confirmation. The billing name and address are printed on your invoice, and the Order ID lets us trace a payment if something goes wrong. Verified payment history is used to check returning subscriber rewards. Billing fields entered on this website are used to prepare checkout and your invoice. The selected payment provider may collect additional billing details.

Prices can vary by billing country. A location provider may process your IP address to estimate your country. Your final total and renewal terms are shown before approval. See subscription terms for renewal and cancellation information.

06 / PRIVACY

Downloads and your email

We verify your email before account access or setup downloads. Your verified email, verification date and account identifier are kept in private account records to provide downloads, licensing and service messages.

Setup download links are temporary and issued after account verification. Installing the toolkit requires a valid access code. Contact AJOY through our contact form with questions about your account records.

07 / PRIVACY

Storage, providers and security

Your browser stores a sign-in token (valid for 30 days), a random browser identifier, site preferences such as light or dark theme, and, for up to 30 minutes, the billing details you type during checkout. After you pay, a copy is kept in that browser tab for up to 2 hours so the confirmation page can print them on your receipt; closing the tab clears it. The website sets no cookies and includes no advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes delivery and security logs. Optional contact attachments are stored in a separate private GitHub repository, with private links kept alongside your message. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Supabase provides licensing infrastructure, the secure checkout gateway and private installer storage. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Google Sheets and Apps Script store private account, checkout, order, invoice and contact-message records, including attachment links, and send verification and service emails. Google Drive hosts the optional FFmpeg archive download. Google privacy policy.
  • PayPal processes payments and subscriptions. PayPal privacy statement.
  • Razorpay processes INR payments and subscriptions where enabled for your country. It receives payment details; we receive payment, invoice and subscription identifiers and status. Razorpay privacy policy.
  • country.is and GeoJS process IP-based country lookups for regional availability and pricing.
  • ExchangeRate-API supplies public exchange rates for local-currency estimates. Your browser sends a standard network request; account, billing and payment details are not sent to this rate service. ExchangeRate-API privacy policy.

Providers may process information outside your country under applicable protections and their terms. We use access controls and secure connections to protect service records; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

08 / PRIVACY

How long information stays

We keep information for as long as needed to administer licenses and accounts, answer support requests, secure the service and meet legal obligations. Sign-in codes expire after 2 minutes. Account, order, invoice and payment records are kept while your account exists, and accounting, dispute and security records may need to remain after a subscription ends. Provider logs follow the relevant service’s retention settings.

Signing out ends your sign-in on our server as well as in your browser. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes saved preferences but does not delete server records. To delete them yourself, sign in, open your account and choose Delete my account and data (it needs a fresh emailed code, and a subscription with access left must end first). It deletes your account, trial, contact and review records; order, payment and invoice records kept for accounting lose your email, name, company, address and messages. You can also use our contact form to request deletion of information linked to your account or license.

Contact attachments may remain in repository history or provider backups until those copies are removed under the provider’s retention processes. Privacy requests include a review of those copies where applicable.

09 / PRIVACY

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. If a use relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Use our contact form with the request and enough detail to identify the relevant account or license. We may ask for proportionate verification before sharing or changing records. Legal duties, security needs or others’ rights may limit a request; we will explain where that applies.

You can decline to enter billing details or contact support. Activation requires the license and device information needed to validate access.

We will update this notice when our handling changes and show the revision date at the top. Material changes will be brought to your attention where required by law. We will update the relevant notice before introducing a new use of personal information.

For privacy, billing-data or licensing questions: contact form.
