Details

Good to know.

Which Maya versions work?

Autodesk Maya 2023 and newer, on Windows.

Can I assign hotkeys?

Yes. Right-click any tool in the Ajoy Tools menu or shelf and choose Assign Hotkey. Maya tells you if the combination is already in use.

What else do I need?

The fbxmaya plug-in for FBX export and AbcExport for Alembic export. The Audio Exporter needs FFmpeg, which is not bundled.

How do I remove it?

Choose Ajoy Tools > Uninstall and confirm. Your settings, presets and exported files are kept.
