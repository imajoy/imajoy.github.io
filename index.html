02The shelf

Fourteen tools. One shelf.

Both toolkits, laid out the way Maya would show them. Point at a tool to inspect it, click to open its guide.

OutlinerDisplay   Show   Help

Wrapline Free · 4 tools

  • SNIK / FK SnapAnimationMatch IK and FK poses on a limb without the arm jumping.
  • MSMulti SwapperAnimationMove animation onto locators, edit it in world space, then send it back.
  • UVUDIM PreviewScenePreview UDIM textures in the viewport at the quality you choose.
  • LPLayer Prefix RemoverSceneStrip or replace a prefix on display and render layers in one go.

Wrapline_Pro Pro · 10 tools

  • IKAdvanced IK FK SwitchRiggingSwitch Advanced Skeleton limbs over a frame, a range or the whole timeline.
  • ABAlembic ExporterExportCache characters and cameras with checked options and versioned names.
  • AUAudio ExporterExportPull 48 kHz stereo WAV audio out of a batch of video shots.
  • DDDrag & DropSceneDrop many files into Maya and pick Import, Reference or Bucket for each.
  • FBFBX ExporterExportExport rigs and cameras as named FBX files for every shot.
  • HKHotkey ManagerSceneBack up, preview and apply hotkey sets, with one-click revert.
  • PBPlayblastAnimationCamera playblasts with presets, audio and the exact size you need.
  • RBRebake IK SpaceRiggingMove IK controls to a new parent or world space and keep the motion.
  • RVRevert to Last SavedSceneReopen the last saved scene in one click, with a backup made first.
  • RIRivetRiggingPin a locator to two edges or a point so objects ride the surface.
