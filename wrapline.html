Which versions of Maya does it support?

Autodesk Maya 2023 and newer, on Windows.

What is the difference between Online and Offline?

Both plans need internet for initial installation. Online checks your subscription each time Maya starts and always runs the newest version. Offline works without internet once installed, and you choose when to update from the Wrapline menu.

What happens if I stop paying?

Access is tied to an active subscription. When it ends, your code is turned off and the tools stop working. See the license for the full terms.

Can I assign hotkeys to the tools?

Yes. Right-click any tool in the Wrapline menu or on the shelf and choose Assign Hotkey. Maya tells you if the combination is already in use.

What else do I need installed?

Wrapline’s FBX Exporter and Alembic Exporter are AJOY scripts. They use Maya’s built-in fbxmaya and AbcExport plug-ins respectively. Load those plug-ins through Windows → Settings/Preferences → Plug-in Manager; no separate Maya plug-in download is needed. Audio Exporter alone needs a separate FFmpeg installation, which is not bundled. See the FBX, Alembic and Audio Exporter guides for setup details.

How do yearly savings and returning subscriber rewards work?

Yearly billing saves 20% compared with twelve standard monthly payments. Eligible returning subscribers get 5% off the second monthly payment and 5% off the third, then the monthly reward ends. The first yearly renewal gets an extra 10% off once, on top of the yearly saving. Monthly rewards do not accumulate, and additional licenses do not advance a reward. Eligibility is verified at a new checkout; existing subscriptions keep their agreed schedule. See reward eligibility and offer terms before subscribing.

How do I remove it?

Choose Wrapline, then Uninstall, and confirm. Your settings, presets and exported files are kept.

Is the setup file safe to share?

Yes, it contains no tools and no secrets. It cannot install anything without a valid code.
