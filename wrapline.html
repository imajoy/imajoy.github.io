      Which versions of Maya does it support?

      Autodesk Maya 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, on Windows.

      What is Drag & Drop?

      Drag & Drop is the 10th Wrapline Pro tool, included with Basic and Premium. Drop .ma, .mb, .fbx, .abc, .obj or USD files into the Maya window and choose what happens to each one: Import, Import as SMPL, Reference, Open in New Maya, Add to Bucket or Skip. It starts by itself with Maya, so there is no shelf button; open the Bucket from the Wrapline Pro menu. Everything runs on your computer. Read the Drag & Drop guide.

      Do I get the free Wrapline tools with Wrapline Pro?

      With Premium, yes: all 14 tools are included, the 10 Pro tools plus IK / FK Snap, Multi Swapper, UDIM Preview and Layer Prefix Remover. Basic includes the 10 Pro tools only, so to use the 4 free tools on Basic, also download free Wrapline. It’s free, and both toolkits work side by side in Maya.

      What is the difference between Basic and Premium?

      Both plans need internet for initial installation. Basic checks your subscription each time Maya starts and always runs the newest version. Premium works without internet once installed, and you choose when to update from the Wrapline Pro menu. Premium also includes the 4 Wrapline tools (IK / FK Snap, Multi Swapper, UDIM Preview and Layer Prefix Remover), so all 14 tools install together and work offline.

      What happens if I stop paying?

      Access is tied to an active subscription. When it ends, your code is turned off and the tools stop working. See the license for the full terms.

      Can I assign hotkeys to the tools?

      Yes. Right-click any tool in the Wrapline Pro menu or on the shelf and choose Assign Hotkey. Maya tells you if the combination is already in use.

      What else do I need installed?

      Wrapline Pro’s FBX Exporter and Alembic Exporter are AJOY scripts. They use Maya’s built-in fbxmaya and AbcExport plug-ins respectively. Load those plug-ins through Windows → Settings/Preferences → Plug-in Manager; no separate Maya plug-in download is needed. Audio Exporter alone needs a separate FFmpeg installation, which is not bundled. See the FBX, Alembic and Audio Exporter guides for setup details.

      How do yearly savings and returning subscriber rewards work?

      Your first yearly subscription of each plan saves 15% (Basic) or 20% (Premium) compared with twelve standard monthly payments; a later yearly subscription on the same plan is charged the regular yearly price. Eligible returning subscribers get 5% off the second monthly payment and 5% off the third, then the monthly reward ends. A second yearly subscription gets an extra 10% off the regular yearly price, once. Monthly rewards do not accumulate, and additional licenses do not advance a reward. Eligibility is verified at a new checkout; existing subscriptions keep their agreed schedule. See reward eligibility and offer terms before subscribing.

      How do I remove it?

      Choose Wrapline Pro, then Uninstall, and confirm. Your settings, presets and exported files are kept.

      Is the setup file safe to share?

      Yes, it contains no tools and no secrets. It cannot install anything without a valid code.
