The shelf

Fourteen tools. One shelf.

Every tool removes one slow job from a real shot. Open any of them to see its guide.

Wrapline Free 4 tools · free for everyone

  • SNAnimationIK / FK SnapMatch IK and FK poses on a limb without the arm jumping.
  • MSAnimationMulti SwapperMove animation onto locators, edit it in world space, then send it back.
  • UVSceneUDIM PreviewPreview UDIM textures in the viewport at the quality you choose.
  • LPSceneLayer Prefix RemoverStrip or replace a prefix on display and render layers in one go.

Wrapline Pro 10 tools · Basic and Premium

  • IKRiggingAdvanced IK FK SwitchSwitch Advanced Skeleton limbs over a frame, a range or the whole timeline.
  • ABExportAlembic ExporterCache characters and cameras with checked options and versioned names.
  • AUExportAudio ExporterPull 48 kHz stereo WAV audio out of a batch of video shots.
  • DDSceneDrag & DropDrop many files into Maya and pick Import, Reference or Bucket for each.
  • FBExportFBX ExporterExport rigs and cameras as named FBX files for every shot.
  • HKSceneHotkey ManagerBack up, preview and apply hotkey sets, with one-click revert.
  • PBAnimationPlayblastCamera playblasts with presets, audio and the exact size you need.
  • RBRiggingRebake IK SpaceMove IK controls to a new parent or world space and keep the motion.
  • RVSceneRevert to Last SavedReopen the last saved scene in one click, with a backup made first.
  • RIRiggingRivetPin a locator to two edges or a point so objects ride the surface.
