Animation

Multi Swapper

Copy animated objects onto locators, edit their world-space motion and transfer it back. Smart baking uses the frames already keyed instead of filling every frame with new keys.

FREE Part of Wrapline, free for everyone.

BUILT FOR THE WAY YOU WORK

Take the motion into world space. Bring it back cleanly.

World-space editsExisting keyframesTranslate or rotate
01

Copy Anim

Create one locator for each selected object, copy its world-space motion on existing keyed frames, then clear the object’s keys.

02

Edit on the locators

Adjust the animation away from the original objects before sending it back.

03

Choose what to transfer

Send Translates, Rotates or Both back to the original objects.

04

Keep the unfinished half

If you transfer only translation or rotation, the locator remains until the other component is transferred too.

GOOD TO KNOW

A detail worth keeping in mind.

Copy Anim clears the original objects’ keys after copying motion to locators. Keep the locators until the components you need are transferred back.
