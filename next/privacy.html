Billing and subscriptions

Billing details are used to prepare your order, determine applicable pricing and taxes, and deliver your license and receipt. While payments are unavailable, the billing form does not submit or store these details. Once enabled, the selected payment processor will handle payment details on its secure checkout. Ajoy does not collect card numbers on this website. Billing details may be retained when needed for invoices, license administration and applicable accounting requirements. Contact Ajoy to request access, correction, or deletion subject to those requirements.

Pricing and country

Prices may vary by billing country. An approximate country derived from your network connection can be used to display a price. When server pricing is enabled, the server may send your IP address to api.country.is for this lookup. Your billing details confirm the final price and applicable taxes before you pay.

Download verification

Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify download requests. Verification data, which may include your IP address and browser signals, is processed by Cloudflare. Installer downloads use a temporary download link. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Storage and contact

The site stores your theme preference in your browser. Billing details are not written to local storage or included in page URLs. Supabase hosts the server functions and may process service logs according to its configured retention. For privacy requests or questions about billing records, email contactmy3dart@gmail.com.
