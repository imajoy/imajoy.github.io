Reward eligibility and offer terms

Your first yearly subscription of each plan saves 15% (Basic) or 20% (Premium) compared with twelve standard monthly payments. A later yearly subscription on the same plan, from the same account, is charged the regular yearly price. You pay annually; the monthly equivalent is a comparison.

Eligible returning subscribers receive 5% off the second monthly payment and 5% off the third, then the monthly reward ends. These discounts do not accumulate. The second annual payment (first yearly renewal) receives an extra 10% off the regular yearly price, once. Later yearly payments are charged at the regular yearly price.

Rewards apply to a new checkout after email ownership and PayPal paid history are verified. Already-approved subscriptions retain their agreed billing schedule. Each reward is available once per verified customer; additional computer licenses do not advance it.

Only confirmed full paid billing periods qualify. Pending, failed, refunded and prorated charges do not advance a reward. At most one full payment per subscription per calendar billing month or year counts; duplicate invoices do not advance eligibility. A consumed reward is not reissued after a refund.

Loyalty rewards and coupons do not stack. Coupons apply only after validation for your plan, billing period, country and quantity, and their terms may exclude other savings. A coupon replaces any returning subscriber reward on that checkout, can be used once per account, and does not apply to the free trial. Review the confirmed total and future billing schedule before approving payment.
