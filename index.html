About AJOY

Tools made by someone who uses the software.

AJOY is a growing collection of scripts, tools, and workflow utilities for 3D artists and animators. The focus is simple: take the little jobs that interrupt creative work and turn them into reliable shortcuts.

Starting with Maya, the toolkit is built to expand into more areas of animation, rigging, and general 3D production.
